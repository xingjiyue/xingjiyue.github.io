Central stars of planetary nebulae (CSPNe) are difficult to identify because they are often faint and embedded in bright nebulae. This project develops a systematic data-driven approach to CSPN candidate selection.

## Approach

- Cross-matched the **HASH** (Hong Kong/AAO/Strasbourg Hα) planetary nebula catalogue with **PanSTARRS** and **DECaPS DR2** wide-field surveys
- Quantified astrometric offsets between catalogue positions and candidate central stars
- Used **Gaia DR3** parallaxes and proper motions for validation of identified candidates
- Ongoing: extending to **multimodal** selection using IPHAS, VPHAS+, and Gaia photometry

## Results

The cross-matching pipeline identified CSPN candidates with quantified confidence, revealing systematic positional offsets in earlier catalogue entries. The multimodal extension aims to improve completeness and purity by combining information across multiple survey passbands.
