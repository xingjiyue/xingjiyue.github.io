I am an MPhil student in Physics at [The Chinese University of Hong Kong](https://www.phy.cuhk.edu.hk/). My research bridges cosmology, interpretable machine learning, and Bayesian data analysis — building scalable computational tools to extract reliable signal from complex data.

**Research focus:** large-scale structure statistics, interpretability of deep learning models for astronomy, photoionization-model inference from integral-field spectroscopy, and multimodal survey-data pipelines.

I enjoy working at the intersection of physics insight and rigorous data methodology — deriving methods, implementing high-performance code, and testing behavior against simulations and observations.

## Highlights
======
- **Fast 3PCF algorithm:** Developed an O(N log N) three-point correlation function pipeline in C++/OpenMP, validated on MDPL2 simulations with &gt;10&sup8; particles.
- **Interpretable radio-galaxy ML:** Built a CNN+LIME workflow for FR-DEEP morphology classification, published at IEEE URSI GASS 2023 and under revision at ApJS.
- **Cloudy + MaNGA inference:** Parallelized photoionisation model grids and used Bayesian comparison to identify revised models that halve the sulphur-line discrepancy.
- **Gaia-based CSPNe selection:** Cross-matched HASH with PanSTARRS and DECaPS DR2 to identify central-star candidates, with ongoing multimodal extension.
