This conference proceeding introduces a local model-interpretation routine for deep-learning-based radio-galaxy morphology classification. The method uses LIME to generate local explanations and visual diagnostics for CNN predictions.

## Key contributions

- Developed a reusable LIME-based interpretation routine for radio-galaxy CNN classifiers
- Generated superpixel-segmented saliency maps for radio survey images
- Connected local explanations with physical radio structures such as jets, lobes, and hotspots
- Provided the proof-of-concept foundation for the later FR-DEEP interpretability manuscript
