Presented recent results on the fast O(N log N) three-point correlation function pipeline, covering the in-situ convolution formalism, C++/OpenMP implementation, and validation against MDPL2 and Quijote simulations. Discussed ongoing extensions to higher-order statistics and sparse-tracer regimes.
