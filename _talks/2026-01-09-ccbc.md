Presented the fast O(N log N) three-point correlation-function pipeline for large-scale structure analysis.

## Talk focus

- Reformulated binned pair-counting as an in-situ convolution problem
- Connected the estimator to binning-corrected perturbation-theory predictions
- Implemented the measurement pipeline in C++ with OpenMP parallelism
- Validated the method on MDPL2 and Quijote simulations
- Discussed extensions to sparse tracers, filter-scale choices, and higher-order statistics
