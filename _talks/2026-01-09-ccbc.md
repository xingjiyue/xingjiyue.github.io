This talk examined how photoionisation models can be evaluated against multiple emission-line diagnostics at the same time, using MaNGA star-forming spaxels and Cloudy model grids.

## Talk focus

- Why agreement in a classical two-dimensional diagram does not guarantee joint agreement across all lines
- Reprojected three-dimensional diagnostic spaces for comparing model surfaces with observed spaxel distributions
- KDE, RBF interpolation, and quantitative model–data distances
- Bayesian inference for metallicity and ionisation parameter
