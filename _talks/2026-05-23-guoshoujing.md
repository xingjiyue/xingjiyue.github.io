Presented the Cloudy plus MaNGA photoionisation-modelling pipeline, focusing on the 3D line-ratio reprojection method and Bayesian inference workflow. Reported results showing standard models over-predict [S III] by a factor of three, and described a revised model configuration that halves the discrepancy while preserving agreement in standard diagnostics.
