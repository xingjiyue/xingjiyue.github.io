Presented my current MPhil work on the [S III] discrepancy in star-forming galaxies, using MaNGA integral-field spectroscopy and Cloudy photoionisation models.

## Talk focus

- Classical optical diagnostics can be reproduced by standard model grids
- Adding [S III]-sensitive diagnostics reveals a systematic model-data tension that is not obvious in traditional 2D BPT projections
- 3D diagnostic spaces, KDE summaries, RBF interpolation, and Bayesian inference were used to compare MaNGA spaxel distributions with Cloudy grids
- The current interpretation is that abundance-only changes are unlikely to be a complete explanation; the ionising SED and model physics remain leading possibilities
